Coworking in Belo Horizonte: a coffee shop built for laptops.

Working from Belo Horizonte? CafeWorking is a coffee shop built for laptops: fast wi-fi, a power outlet at every table, meeting rooms by the hour and no side-eye if you stay all afternoon. Two locations — Luxemburgo and Estoril.

Talk to us on WhatsApp → See the space

What it is

CafeWorking is part coffee shop, part coworking space. You can come in for a single flat white, spend the whole day working from a table, book a meeting room for one hour, or rent a private office by the month. Nobody will rush you out, and every seat is wired for a working day — not for a fifteen-minute stop.

It is run by Grupo Ciatos, a Brazilian accounting and legal group, which means the same address can also serve as your company's registered office if you need one.

What you get

  • Fast wi-fi — dimensioned for video calls with the camera on, not just for checking email.
  • A power outlet at every table. You will not be hunting for one along the wall.
  • Real coffee, made on site, plus food and snacks through the day.
  • Meeting rooms from 4 to 14 people, bookable by the hour, with a TV for presentations.
  • A staffed reception that greets your guests and points them to the right room.
  • Quiet. It is a working environment, so conversations stay at working volume.

Where we are

Both locations are in the south-central business area of Belo Horizonte, close to Belvedere, Gutierrez, Santo Agostinho and the road to Nova Lima.

  • Luxemburgo — Rua Guaicuí, 715. This is the one with the coffee shop, the garden and the outdoor tables. See the location.
  • Estoril — Av. Raja Gabaglia, 2000. A more corporate floor, with glass-walled offices and a view. See the location.

Opening hours

Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 18:00. If you need access outside those hours, ask us — it can usually be arranged for monthly members.

How much it costs

There is no fixed price list, because almost nobody buys the same thing. A day pass, three days a week, a full-time desk, a private office for a team of six, or just a registered business address are very different products. Tell us how you actually work — how many people, how many days, whether you meet clients — and we will send you a price the same day.

Just visiting Belo Horizonte? A day pass is the simplest option, and it includes coffee. Message us on WhatsApp the day before and we will have a table ready.

Business services in English

Through Grupo Ciatos, we also handle company formation, a registered fiscal address, accounting and business legal support in Brazil — useful if you are a foreign founder setting up a Brazilian entity, or a remote worker who needs a local CNPJ. We can walk you through it in English.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I work from CafeWorking for a whole day?

Yes. That is what the space is for. A day pass gives you a table for the full working day, with wi-fi, a power outlet and coffee included. Nobody will ask you to free up the table.

Is there a coffee shop with good wi-fi in Belo Horizonte?

CafeWorking was built exactly for that: real coffee plus internet dimensioned for video calls, with a power outlet at every table. It is in the Luxemburgo neighbourhood, close to Belvedere and the road to Nova Lima.

Do you speak English?

Yes. Our team can help you in English, both at the reception and for business services such as company formation and accounting through Grupo Ciatos.

Can I book a meeting room by the hour?

Yes. Rooms range from 4 to 14 seats and include a TV for presentations, wi-fi and reception support. Message us on WhatsApp with the date, time and number of people.

What are the opening hours?

Monday to Friday, from 8:00 to 18:00. Access outside those hours can usually be arranged for monthly members.

Can I use the address for my Brazilian company?

Yes. A registered fiscal address is one of the services we offer, and it includes mail handling and a real place to receive clients or inspections.
